PIXERIONDEV · LANTERN ISLES

Every step changes the path.

A pixel-art path puzzle about collecting lanterns, shifting bridge stones, and finding a way to the exit. More than 200 puzzles across 17 island regions, with a daily island challenge as you progress.

Support

For questions, bug reports or privacy requests, email pixerion.dev@gmail.com. Please include your device, iOS version, game version and level number when reporting a problem. Never send passwords or payment information.

How do the bridge stones work?

A stone has two matching positions. When you leave the stone, it moves to the other position. Collect every lantern to open the exit. Undo, restart and hints help you try another route.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress and settings are saved on your device. No account or cloud sync is required. Uninstalling or resetting the game can remove local progress.

Ads and reminders

Rewarded ads are optional. Interstitial ads can appear between levels after the introduction. Privacy choices are available in Settings where required. Daily reminders are optional local notifications that can be switched off in the game or iOS Settings.
